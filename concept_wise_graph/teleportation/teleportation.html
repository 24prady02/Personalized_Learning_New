Rules of the concept (14)

Book (SW-1e, p. 168 (pdf 178)): “The primary purpose of the 90-10 rule is to guarantee mixing and eliminate such anomalies.”
Book (SW-1.6, ): “90 per cent of the time the random surfer clicks a random link on the current page”
Rule typeRule
E folk-meaning ruleEveryday "90 percent of the time" reads as a quota (exactly 9 of every 10 moves follow links); in the model it is a probability applied independently on every move, folded into each matrix entry as .90*(link share) + .10/N.
M wrong rule / right ruleTeleportUnneeded: wrong: Without teleporting, the surfer still visits every page. | right: Without the random leap the surfer can get stuck (e.g. in a spider trap with no outgoing links); the 90-10 rule guarantees mixing.
T uneven-application ruleApplies LT (add .10/N to every entry of every row, after the link term) unevenly: e.g. adds it only to entries that have links, or only to the diagonal, so rows sum to less than 1.
N identity ruleA model rule: the random leap of the 90-10 rule, a jump to a page chosen uniformly from all N pages with probability .10.
S declarative rule90 percent of the time the surfer clicks a random link on the current page (each link equally likely) and 10 percent of the time goes directly to a random page (all pages equally likely); in the matrix this adds .10/N to each entry.
LT operational sequencing rulePer entry, the link term .90*counts[i][j]/outDegree[i] is computed first and the leap term .10/N is then added to every entry, including entries with no link and the diagonal.
OP evaluation / result ruleThe leap contributes .10/N to each entry: .02 for tiny.txt (N = 5), so an entry with no link is .02 and the single link of page 0 gives .92.
OS state-transition ruleAdding the leap term raises one entry p by .10/N; summed across a row it adds exactly .10.
R composition rule (with a neighbour)with transition_matrix: Every entry is built as link part plus leap part; the .90 and .10 weights must sum to 1 for each row to remain a distribution.
P plan / role ruleGuarantee mixing: the leap makes every page reachable from every page, so the chain has a unique long-run distribution and spider traps cannot capture the surfer.
C conservation ruleChanging the split to 80-20 or 95-5 keeps rows stochastic and mixing but changes the ranks; removing the leap (100-0) can break mixing.
D error rulerule broken: The leap is needed for mixing. (silent logic error)
D error rulerule broken: Leap and link weights must sum to 1. (silent logic error)
X applicability ruleUse whenever the link graph may not be strongly connected (any real web model) to make the chain mix; the particular 90-10 split is a guess, so it is a modelling parameter, not a fact.
